# ProView TV

A TV player for your own IPTV playlist, built for sports fans. It runs on Android TV and Google TV. **Beta.**

**ProView doesn't include any channels, playlists or subscriptions.** You need your own M3U link or Xtream Codes login from your TV provider, and should only use it with content you have the right to watch.

**Download:** https://github.com/gitzero-io/ProView-releases/releases/latest/download/ProView.apk

It's free to use right now, with no ads and no account. That may change in a future version.

## What it does

- **Your playlist:** M3U links and Xtream Codes logins, including very large playlists. Add one with the remote or from your phone using a QR code.
- **TV guide:** XMLTV listings from your provider or a link you add.
- **Sports Hub:** live scores, followed teams, spoiler-free mode, match details, and links from games to channels in your playlist.
- **Multiview:** watch several channels at once. How many depends on your TV's memory.
- **Reconnecting:** tries again automatically when a stream drops.
- **Search:** across channels, guide listings and teams, plus movies and series unless you use IPTV-only mode.
- **Movies and Series:** browse what's on the streaming services you choose, then open a title in that service's own app. ProView doesn't play these itself.
- **IPTV-only mode:** turn this on in Settings if you just want the player. It hides streaming services, live TV providers, Movies and Series.
- **Basic Mode:** big text and fewer buttons, like a cable box.
- **Parental PIN:** lock categories and channels.
- **In-app updates:** ProView tells you when there's a new version.

Recording isn't available yet.

## Install

You need Android 7.0 or newer on the TV.

1. Install the **Downloader** app from your TV's app store.
2. In Downloader, enter the download link above.
3. Allow Downloader to install unknown apps when asked, then install ProView.
4. Open ProView and follow the setup. Choose **I only use IPTV** if you don't want the streaming features.

## What's been tested

ProView is new, and so far it has only been tested on one real TV, an Onn 4K Pro, plus the Android TV emulator.

- **Fire TV and other TVs:** not tested yet. It may well work, and reports are welcome.
- **Known gaps:** Basic Mode hasn't been tested with very large playlists, and reconnecting hasn't been tested through a real internet outage.
- **Sports Hub data:** it comes from unofficial ESPN endpoints, so it could change or stop working without notice.

## Problems and suggestions
